What's Included

One price. Nothing hidden.

Everything below is covered from the moment we leave the trailhead. Optional gear rentals are yours to pick at checkout.

Tents & sleep systems

Four-season tent per two people, temperature-rated sleeping bag, and an insulated pad. Set up before you arrive.

Three meals a day

Cooked fresh at camp. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free handled — just flag it when you book.

Guide & wilderness medic

A certified guide on every departure, with at least one Wilderness First Responder in the group.

Return transport

Shuttle from the meeting point in Toronto, Vancouver or Jasper, depending on the trip — both directions.

Trip insurance

Accident coverage for every day on trail, active the moment the shuttle pulls away.

Genuinely small groups

Sixteen people, hard cap. Quiet camps, light footprint, and a guide with time for everyone.

Know Before You Go

Camp rules

Six simple things that keep everyone safe and leave the forest exactly as we found it.

Cancellation policy

Full refund up to 10 days before departure, 50% up to 4 days. Weather cancellations are refunded in full, any time.

No payment today

Booking holds your spot for 48 hours. We call to confirm details and arrange payment before anything is charged.

01

Leave no trace

Everything you pack in comes back out with you. No cutting live wood, no exceptions.

02

Fires in rings only

Your guide picks the fire site. It gets fully drowned before anyone sleeps.

03

Quiet after midnight

No speakers after 12. Sound carries further than you'd think out there — and wildlife notices.

04

Kids welcome from 8

On easy-rated trips, with a parent or guardian present the entire time.

05

Tell us about your health

Allergies, medication, past injuries — put it in the booking form. Only the guide and medic ever see it.

06

No alcohol, no firearms

At every site. Breaking this ends your trip immediately, without refund.
